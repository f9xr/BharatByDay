# Dzüko Valley: The flower meadow hidden between two states

Tucked into the fold where Nagaland and Manipur lean into each other, **Dzüko Valley** is a high-altitude meadow that most of Northeast India's own guidebooks forget to mention.

## Why it's a gem

At 2,452 metres, this bowl of rolling grassland is ringed by pine-and-rhododendron ridges and, for a few short weeks in summer, erupts into a sea of wildflowers. Locals call it *Dzükou* — the "dwelling of the spirits" in the Angami language — and you'll understand why on a misty morning.

## Getting there

- **Base:** Viswema (Nagaland), about 25 km from Kohima
- **Trek:** 14–16 km each way; steep climbs, one night at the Dzüko campsite
- **Best time:** June to early September for flowers; December to March for crisp, clear skies

**Know before you go:** The route crosses into Manipur's territory — carry your permit, carry your trash out, and hire a local guide from Viswema.
