# Chand Baori: the stairway to oblivion

An hour from Jaipur, past the turn-off most tour vans never take, the village of Abhaneri hides one of the world's oldest and deepest stepwells — **Chand Baori**, 3,500 symmetrical steps descending 13 storeys into the earth.

## Why it's a gem

Its sheer geometry stops you cold: thousands of perfectly angled stairs form a fractal that seems to fall forever. Built around the 9th century, it was part a water source and part a social hub — kings, farmers, and travellers all came down here. You can't descend anymore, but the view from the rim is worth the detour alone.

## Getting there

- **Location:** Abhaneri, Dausa district, Rajasthan
- **From Jaipur:** ~95 km via NH21; the site is beside the Harshat Mata temple
- **Best time:** October to March; arrive early to beat the tour groups

**Know before you go:** The stepwell is managed by the Archaeological Survey of India — entry is nominal, photography is free, and there's a small museum upstairs.
