Welcome to **भारतByDay**, a daily travel journal for India. One post per day, each about a single place: how to reach it, what it costs, and the best season to go.

The same ten destinations dominate every list, while thousands of good ones get no coverage at all. We write about the ones that don't make the cut: stepwells, high-altitude meadows, coffee estates, street-food stalls. Whether you plan weekend trips or long overland routes, every post gives you enough detail to act on.

## Launching this Independence Day

We launch on **August 15, 2026**, as India marks its **80th Independence Day**, with flag-hoisting ceremonies, cultural programs, and the Prime Minister's national address. A fitting day to start a journal about the Bharat the guidebooks miss.

## Why you can trust every post

Every destination on भारतByDay is researched from primary sources before it is published: Archaeological Survey of India records, forest and wildlife department permits, and local guides. Our [F9XR Editorial Team]({{ "/author/f9xr" | relative_url }}) verifies routes, seasons, and entry requirements, and we correct the record openly when things change.

- **Experience**: reporting drawn from across India's states and union territories.
- **Expertise**: practical logistics, how to get there, when to go, what to carry.
- **Authority**: published under the F9XR masthead, with sources cited in each post.
- **Trust**: an open-access, [open-source project](https://github.com/f9xr/BharatByDay) under a Creative Commons licence.
